Eternal President — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026 · Developer: Volodymyr Alkhymenko (ForeverDreamer) · Contact: gamesmen514@gmail.com

“Eternal President” (the “Game”) is a single-player satirical card game for Android. This policy explains what information the Game handles. We keep it minimal: there are no user accounts, and we do not sell your personal data.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (current run, records, unlocked endings and achievements, settings, daily-streak counter, chosen language) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

2. Advertising

The Game shows ads (interstitial and rewarded) through the Unity LevelPlay mediation SDK and the ad networks connected to it (for example Unity Ads and Google AdMob). These providers may collect and process, according to their own privacy policies: your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and OS information, and ad interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where required, you are asked for consent before personalized ads; you can change your choice in the Game or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Provider policies: Unity, Google.

3. In-app purchases

The optional “Remove ads” purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase was made.

4. Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your score (length of reign) and achievements are sent to Google Play Games Services, which shows them on leaderboards and in your profile according to your Play Games settings. Signing in is optional; the Game works without it.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules a local daily reminder on your device. No data is sent to our servers for this. You can turn it off in Android settings.

6. Analytics

The Game does not currently send gameplay analytics to our servers. If this changes, this policy will be updated before such data is collected.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Your rights and data deletion

We do not hold personal data about you on our own servers. Local data is deleted by uninstalling the Game. For data held by Google (Play Games, Play billing) or ad providers, use their privacy controls. If you are in the EEA/UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data; contact the providers above or write to us at the email below and we will help.

9. Security and changes

We rely on the security of the platforms and providers above. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
